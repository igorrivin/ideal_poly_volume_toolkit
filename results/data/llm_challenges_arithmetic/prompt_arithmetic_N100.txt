Explicitly construct 10 pairwise non-isometric convex ideal polyhedra in hyperbolic 3-space H^3, each with exactly 100 ideal vertices and all triangular faces, such that every polyhedron is arithmetic --- meaning the traces of all elements of the holonomy representation

    rho: pi_1(boundary surface) -> PSL(2, C)

are algebraic integers.

**Why such polyhedra exist.** By a theorem of Rivin (Ann. of Math. 139 (1994), 553--580; see also Adv. Appl. Math. 31 (2003), 242--271, arXiv:math/0200122):

For any combinatorial triangulation T of the 2-sphere realizable as the boundary of a convex ideal polyhedron in H^3, the hyperbolic volume functional V on the space of ideal polyhedra with combinatorial type T is strictly concave on the Rivin polytope (the convex body of valid angle assignments satisfying: angles positive, triangle angle sums = pi, interior vertex angle sums = 2*pi, dihedral angles at interior edges <= pi). Its unique global maximum is a critical point at which all dihedral angles are rational multiples of pi.

When the dihedral angles are rational multiples of pi, the shape parameters z_e = exp(i * theta_e) are roots of unity, so the holonomy matrices have entries in a cyclotomic field and all their traces are algebraic integers. The surface of the polyhedron inherits a complete finite-area hyperbolic metric, making it H^2 / Gamma for a Fuchsian group Gamma < PSL(2, R) that is (conjecturally) arithmetic.

**Construction method.** To produce distinct arithmetic ideal polyhedra:
1. Choose 100 distinct points in C union {infinity} (the boundary of the upper half-space model of H^3). Different random choices of points generically yield different Delaunay triangulations and hence different combinatorial types.
2. Compute the Delaunay triangulation of these points on S^2 (via stereographic projection).
3. Optimize the vertex positions to maximize the hyperbolic volume V = sum over triangles T of (Lambda(alpha_T) + Lambda(beta_T) + Lambda(gamma_T)), where Lambda is the Lobachevsky function Lambda(theta) = -integral from 0 to theta of ln|2 sin t| dt.
4. At the volume maximum, all dihedral angles are rational multiples of pi and the holonomy is arithmetic.

Different initial point configurations lead to different combinatorial triangulations, giving non-isometric polyhedra after optimization.

**Output format.** For each polyhedron provide:
  1. The ideal vertices z_1, ..., z_{100} as complex numbers in C.
  2. The face triangulation as a list of vertex-index triples [i, j, k] (0-indexed).
  3. The hyperbolic volume.

Return your answer as a JSON array of objects:

[
  {
    vertices: [[re_1, im_1], [re_2, im_2], ...],
    triangles: [[i, j, k], ...],
    volume: <float>
  },
  ...
]
